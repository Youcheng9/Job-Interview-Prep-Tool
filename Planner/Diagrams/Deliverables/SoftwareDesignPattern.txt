Recommendation

  Use a Service Layer pattern for this project.

  Right now the backend routers are doing HTTP handling, DB access,
  scoring orchestration, and AI feedback workflow all in one place.
  The clearest example is backend/routers/scoring.py:1, where the
  route validates input, creates Answer, computes scores, stores
  Score, manages pending AI feedback state, and launches background
  work. That is a service, not just a controller.

  A Service Layer fits this repo because it gives you a clean
  split:

  - Routers/controllers: request/response only
  - Services: interview scoring, question flow, auth, history
    assembly
  - Repositories/data access: SQLAlchemy queries
  - Domain/ML helpers: scorer, embedder, feedback agent

  That structure matches what the app already wants to be:

  - backend/routers/auth.py:1, backend/routers/history.py:1, and
    backend/routers/questions.py:1 all contain business logic that
    will keep growing.
  - backend/ml/scorer.py:1 and backend/feedback_agent.py:1 are
    already service-like dependencies waiting to be orchestrated
    from one layer up.

  A practical target shape would be:

  backend/
    services/
      auth_service.py
      question_service.py
      scoring_service.py
      history_service.py
    repositories/
      question_repository.py
      answer_repository.py
      user_repository.py
      score_repository.py

  Example responsibility split:

  - ScoringService.submit_answer(...)
  - ScoringService.generate_ai_feedback(...)
  - QuestionService.list_questions(...)
  - HistoryService.get_user_history(...)
